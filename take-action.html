5 things that you can do today to help coral reefs and beyond

1. Use your voice — Demand climate solutions, vote in local and national elections, and talk to others about climate change and solutions.

  1. Example: Post on social media.

2. Donate to conservation, restoration and climate research (for coral reefs or otherwise).

  1. Coral Restoration Foundation: coralrestoration.org
  2. Adopt a coral @ Perry Institute for Marine Science: perryinstitute.org/donate

3. Volunteer locally — The climate crisis is a global issue and local efforts help to move the needle!

  1. Los Angeles
    1. Friends of the LA River: folar.org/get-involved
    2. L.A. Works: laworks.com/get-started
    3. Friends of Ballona Wetlands: ballonafriends.org/volunteer
    4. Friends of Griffith Park: friendsofgriffithpark.org/volunteer
    5. Silver Lake Reservoir Conservancy: silverlakereservoirs.org/volunteer
    6. Informal: Beach cleanup

4. Incorporate small lifestyle changes — Sustainability starts with us.

  1. Move your money to smaller local credit unions, and away from megabanks.
    1. U.S. Banks League Table
    2. Wells Fargo sustainability profile
  2. Buy second hand (good for your wallet, and good for the world).
  3. Participate in the FREE city-wide compost program.
  4. Reduce single-use plastic (but also single-use anything, really).
  5. Eat local and reduce meat consumption (good for our community, good for the world).
  6. Take public transport, bike or carpool.

5. Believe in a better future and never give up.

It's easy to be overwhelmed and sad and feel like nothing will ever change. But every little bit of carbon we don’t emit and every bit of warming we stop is so impactful.
